Our Story

Started with one pot, one porch, and too many beans.

Amber & Ash began in 2015 as a weekend hobby — roasting green beans in a cast-iron skillet on a back porch, just to see if we could make something better than what was sold down the street.

Nine years later, we still roast in small batches, still greet regulars by name, and still believe a good cup of coffee is worth slowing down for.
